      KLN 94 training syllabus

      Official Pilot's Guide

      Use the trainer to practice the real interaction model: cursor off selects pages, cursor on edits fields, and the pulled inner knob scans waypoints. Procedure data in this site remains explicitly marked TRAINING.

      1. GPS awarenessNAV pages, position, distance, bearing, groundspeed and nearest-airport use.
      2. Direct-ToD→, enter the identifier, review the waypoint, then press ENT again to activate.
      3. More than Direct-ToAPT, VOR, NDB, INT and USR pages; nearest and user-waypoint workflows.
      4. Model-T approachesChoose the IAF that matches the arrival sector and understand IAF, IF, FAF and MAP roles.
      5. DPs and STARsUse PROC to choose the procedure and transition and place the fixes into FPL 0.
      6. Flight plansCreate, edit and activate FPL 0; use LEG sequencing and scan upcoming waypoints.
      7. GPS for IFRCheck database currency, RAIM integrity, CDI source and automatic sensitivity changes.
      8. RNAV chart formatMatch chart fixes and LNAV minimums to the procedure waypoints loaded in the unit.
      9. Vectors and overlaysPractice VTF and distinguish GPS overlays from stand-alone RNAV approaches.
      10. Fly the approachLOADED → APR ARM → APR ACTV → MAP suspend; use OBS/LEG correctly and command the miss.
      Modeled limits: LNAV lateral guidance only; no LPV glidepath. An expired database or lost RAIM blocks/downgrades approach use. Automatic sequencing stops at the MAP.
